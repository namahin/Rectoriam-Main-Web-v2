Rectoriam Legal & Compliance

Privacy & Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

Jurisdiction Notice: The Rectoriam App operates under the legal jurisdiction of Canada. This policy complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), global Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards, and Google Play's strict Developer Data Safety Policies.

At Rectoriam, we prioritize absolute transparency regarding how your data is collected, utilized, and protected across our digital wallet, micro-investment platform, and native points ecosystem. Please read this policy carefully to understand our technical and operational data practices.

1. Financial Features & Data Collection

To provide you with a fully functional investment and wallet experience, we collect specific data points directly tied to the features you use within the app:

  • Account & Identification: Name, Email, and your unique User ID (e.g., REC100022).
  • Wallet Top-Up: When adding funds via bKash, Nagad, or Crypto, we collect transaction IDs, deposit amounts, and optionally, uploaded payment proof slips to verify the transaction. We DO NOT store your mobile money PINs or crypto private keys.
  • Investments & Assets: We track your portfolio allocations across sectors (e.g., Tech, Crude Oil, Green Energy), monitoring your invested amounts, growth rates, expected return dates, and accumulated profits.
  • P2P Transfers: If you transfer funds internally using a Receiver ID, we process the transfer amount, recipient details, applied promo/coupon codes, and require OTP verification for security. We log these transactions for fraud prevention.
  • Withdrawals & Balances: To process cash-outs from your Available Balance, we collect your designated payment method details. All withdrawal requests are subject to strict KYC compliance.
  • Rector Coins & Referrals: We track your daily visit bonuses, conversion history (Rector Coins to USD), and referral links/invitees to accurately distribute your rewards.

2. Mandatory Identity Verification (KYC)

To comply with Canadian and international financial regulations, and to process your Withdrawals securely, you must complete ID Verification. We collect:

  • Document Type & Number: Details of your valid government-issued ID.
  • Document Images/PDFs: Front and back images of your ID, uploaded directly through the app. These files are highly encrypted and used solely for identity verification and AML compliance.

3. SDK Integrations & App Permissions Explained

The Rectoriam App is built using the Flutter framework and integrates specific third-party SDKs to function securely. We declare the following technical data practices to comply with Google Play policies:

SDK / Package Used Data Accessed & Justification

4. How We Protect Your Data

Your financial security is our top priority. All API communications are secured via HTTPS/TLS encryption. Sensitive data, including uploaded KYC documents and wallet transaction logs, are stored on secure servers with restricted access limited only to authorized compliance officers.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your data. Information is shared strictly under the following circumstances:

  • Payment Processors: Necessary data is shared with mobile financial services (bKash/Nagad) or crypto nodes to process your Top-Ups and Withdrawals.
  • Legal Compliance: We will disclose transaction records or KYC data if explicitly required by Canadian law enforcement, FINTRAC, or international regulatory bodies to combat fraud and money laundering.

6. Data Deletion & Account Closure

In accordance with Google Play’s Data Safety guidelines, you possess the right to request the deletion of your Rectoriam account. You can request a full account wipe or partial data removal (e.g., clearing app cache or KYC documents) by visiting our Account Deletion Portal.

Note: For legal and regulatory compliance, records of completed financial transactions (Investments, Top-Ups, Withdrawals) may be retained for a mandatory period even after account deletion.
