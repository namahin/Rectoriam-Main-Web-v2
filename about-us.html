About Rectoriam

Democratizing global investments through technology, security, and strict regulatory compliance.

Our Mission

At Rectoriam, we believe that wealth creation should not be limited by geographical borders or traditional financial barriers. Our mission is to provide a seamless, secure, and accessible platform where individuals can manage their digital wallets and participate in micro-investment opportunities across high-growth sectors like Technology, Green Energy, and Commodities.

"Start where you are. Use what you have. Do what you can."

Jurisdiction & Regulatory Commitment

Rectoriam operates under the strict legal jurisdiction of Canada. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of financial integrity and data protection.

  • Data Protection: We strictly adhere to the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), ensuring user data is collected transparently and stored securely.
  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML): To prevent financial crimes, our platform enforces mandatory Know Your Customer (KYC) protocols before authorizing withdrawals. This ensures a safe ecosystem for all investors.
  • Platform Integrity: We comply with international digital store policies, including Google Play's Developer Policies, to guarantee user safety, transparent data handling, and secure software architecture.

What We Offer

Smart Digital Wallet

Securely deposit and hold funds using localized gateways (e.g., bKash, Nagad) or global Cryptocurrency networks. Your assets are protected by enterprise-grade encryption.

Diversified Portfolios

Subscribe to 15-day or 30-day investment plans in sectors like AI, Real Estate, and Crude Oil, and monitor your projected yields directly from the dashboard.

P2P Ecosystem

Transfer funds instantly to other verified Rectoriam users securely using OTP (One-Time Password) authentication.

Rector Coins Rewards

Earn internal points (Rector Coins) for active engagement and successful referrals, which can be converted based on platform metrics.

Our Commitment to Transparency

Transparency is the foundation of trust. We explicitly define how we operate, how yields are calculated, and how user data is utilized. We do not sell user data. All third-party SDKs integrated into our mobile applications are used solely to enhance security (e.g., local encrypted storage) and improve functionality (e.g., secure network calls).
